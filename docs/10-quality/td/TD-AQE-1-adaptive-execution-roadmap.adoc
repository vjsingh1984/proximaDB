== Why this TD

ADR-056 commits ProximaDB to the **predicate-pushdown school** as the lead adaptive-execution strategy (re-plan school deferred — needs a stage/materialization abstraction we don't have, and is shuffle-centric/irrelevant single-node). This TD breaks that decision into trackable, sequenced slices so the work can be estimated, dependency-ordered, and progress-tracked across sessions. The research basis (Spark/Trino/DuckDB/ClickHouse/Impala/Hive/Polars/Pandas survey) is preserved in ADR-056 §4+§8.

== Sequence + dependencies (the critical path)

The dependency graph (→ = unblocks):

```
AQE-S1 (bloom default-ON) ──────────────────────────────► [qa-gate verifies bytes_read]
AQE-S2 (observability) ─────► AQE-S4 (build/probe swap) ─► AQE-S10 (cache-composition)
AQE-S3 (build min/max) ──────────────────────────────► [compounds with S1 at the scan]
AQE-S5 (sort-on-materialize) ───────────────────────────► [force-multiplies S1/S3]
AQE-S6 (PREWHERE/PAX) ──────────────────────────────────► [vector+filter workload]
TD-OLAP-11 (join) ──► AQE-S9 (materialization boundary) ─► AQE-S7 (MemoryPool) ─► AQE-S8 (grace_hash)
                                                                                  └─► AQE-S12 (re-plan loop, far future)
AQE-S11 (wait-time dial) ──────────────────────────────► [independent; cheap]
```

**Recommended execution order (leverage × unblocked-dependents first):**
. **AQE-S1** (now) — cheapest, highest-leverage, correctness-neutral.
. **AQE-S2** (next) — unblocks S4; the measurement substrate.
. **AQE-S11** (cheap, independent) — make the wait-time tunable.
. **AQE-S3** then **AQE-S5** — compound with S1 at the scan (the scan-avoidance stack).
. **AQE-S9** (design directive on #779) — preserve the re-plan option while the join is being built.
. **AQE-S6** — the vector+filter win (PAX PREWHERE).
. **AQE-S4** (needs S2) — the free AQE build/probe swap.
. **AQE-S7 → AQE-S8** — robustness (with TD-OLAP-11 spill).
. **AQE-S10** (needs S4) — cache-composition, once plans are adaptive.
. Tier 3 (S12/S13/S14) — only when distributed execution lands.
