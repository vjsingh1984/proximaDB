== Why

The original problem and measured negative result remain valid: sign-bit ordering did not make
VOE block pruning selective enough. The surviving asset is the compaction-time canonical-f32
PCA+IVF plan. Its current payoff is locality and TD-RDSTRAT-8 Region-A0 probing, not revival of
VOE radius pruning. Short-lived coalesced L0 uses model-free SQ8-Morton.

== Scope

* At compaction for `AppendBulk` collections: train PCA + IVF (k-means) on PCA-projected fp32 and
  re-cluster through the canonical compacted-segment writer.
* L0 flush uses SQ8-Morton (no trained model). `Churn` pins compaction training OFF.
* **Prerequisite: hoist `kmeans_clustering` from `crates/modalities/proximadb-quantization-kernel`
  to a foundation crate** — storage/compaction must not depend on a modality kernel (layering
  guard). Reuse it; do not reimplement Lloyd.
* Order IVF cells by their centroid's Hilbert code so same-region blocks are physically contiguous
  (coalescing-friendly).
