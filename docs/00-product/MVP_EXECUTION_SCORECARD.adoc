= MVP Execution and Design-Partner Scorecard
:toc: left
:icons: font

== North-star outcome

A new AI application team loads its own typed context records, retrieves them through metadata and
vector search, and keeps the same deployment in recurring use. Repository activity alone is not a
market outcome.

== Funnel

[cols="1,2,2", options="header"]
|===
| Stage | Instrumented event | MVP target

| Discover
| Unique visitors who reach the context-serving quickstart
| Measure baseline; no vanity target

| Activate
| Successful `scripts/mvp_smoke.py`-equivalent roundtrip against user-owned data
| 50 external activations

| Retain
| Same deployment performs writes and filtered searches in each of seven consecutive days
| 20% of activated deployments

| Design partner
| Named team completes a problem interview, workload capture, benchmark replay, and go/no-go review
| 5 active partners

| Evidence
| Public, reproducible case study with workload, configuration, limitations, and economics
| 3 case studies

| Ecosystem
| Non-founder maintainer who reviews or owns a release-gating area
| 2 maintainers
|===

ProximaDB does not phone home from the OSS engine or smoke script. Activation and retention are
counted only from explicit design-partner participation, opt-in hosted-service telemetry, or a user
voluntarily attaching the non-sensitive smoke/benchmark report. The public intake form is
`.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/design-partner.yml`; it asks for workload shape and explicitly forbids
credentials or customer data.

== Design-partner contract

Each partner brings one real context-serving workload. ProximaDB supplies the canonical v2 adapter,
query-scoped physical-cost trace, and a reproducible result bundle. Together they decide:

. which application-side join or operational store is being removed;
. the correctness boundary, including metadata isolation and recovery expectations;
. target record count, write rate, filtered-query mix, latency, and cost envelope;
. which physical dimension dominates: object requests, bytes, cache, compute, network, or policy;
. whether the evidence supports adoption, a scoped architectural change, or rejection of the use case.

Partner requests do not become roadmap items solely because they are requested. They must strengthen
the canonical record/trust corridor, serve more than one partner, and preserve standards outside the
engine. Distributed execution requires three independent partner workloads that cannot be served by
single-node compute over decoupled storage.

== Weekly operating review

[cols="1,2", options="header"]
|===
| Review | Decision

| Trust corridor
| Any P0/P1 wrong-result, durability, isolation, install, or release-asset failure stops expansion.

| Activation
| If users do not finish the quickstart, fix packaging and error messages before adding capabilities.

| Retention
| If activated users do not return, interview for missing workflow value before optimizing benchmarks.

| Economics
| Choose work from measured object/network/cache/compute cost, not engine novelty.

| Scope
| A Beta surface graduates only with recovery, conformance, observability, and design-partner evidence.
|===

The numeric targets are also recorded in
link:MVP_TRUST_CORRIDOR.toml[MVP_TRUST_CORRIDOR.toml] so planning automation and narrative cannot
silently diverge.
