== Findings

. **GET-parity A1 pair is measurement-perfect**: GETs, bytes, recall AND
   result-identity hash all identical across arms; wave observably fired
   (297 rounds, width 8) vs the sequential arm's 0/0.
. **Measured win: 1.61× aggregate p50, 1.54× cold-cohort** — below the 2×
   acceptance. Measured reason: compute (~0.9 s of 517k-row rank+decode)
   dominates this bed's p50; with baseline I/O ≈ 14.4×RTT collapsing to
   ≈1–2 waves, the bed's achievable ceiling lands near 1.6×.
. **At real-cloud RTT the ≥2× remains reachable** (I/O share grows with
   RTT; waved I/O stays at 1–2 waves). The criterion is re-scoped to a
   real-cloud-latency bed rather than abandoned.
. **Split arm preserves identity when quiet** (a2/1 = same hash), proving
   the per-chunk feed correctness; its GETs also stayed 14.40 (the
   whole-region fallback did not fire on this bed at settle geometry —
   the split lever is exercised on scattered-survivor beds, cf. round 3).
