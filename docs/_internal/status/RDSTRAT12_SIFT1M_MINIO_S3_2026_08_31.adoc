== Headline

The read_ranges wave mechanism is **backend-neutral and identity-preserving on an S3-shaped
backend**: on byte-identical same-bed pairs the waved arm fired observably (2.1-2.2 rounds/query,
width 8 vs 0/0 sequential) at **exact GET parity**, identical recall, and **identical
result-identity hashes**. But the **>=2x crossover did NOT reproduce on this bed**: the ratio caps
at 1.17-1.64x across 5.5-150 ms RTT, because the post-collapse v1 layout leaves roughly half of
the per-query GET cascade outside the waved regions and the toxic path couples RTT into bulk
transfer. The >=2x result remains demonstrated on the Azurite/v3-layout bed (#1794); the two
campaigns measure different format regimes (see "Regime delta").

Wave observability: every A1 arm `read_ranges_fetch_rounds` 2.10-2.16 rounds/query,
`max_inflight` 8; every A0 arm 0/0. All arms ran `PROXIMADB_READ_RANGES_INFLIGHT=8` (A1) or unset (A0);

The rtt50 pair mixed two different settled geometries (A0: L1 250k/7 cells + L2 750k/22 cells;
A1: L1 500k/15c + L2 500k/15c) — the compaction outcome is bistable across arms. Its GET mismatch
and identity divergence are therefore **bed-geometry artifacts, not wave-correctness signals**;
the three same-bed pairs carry the correctness proof. Within the mixed pair recall still matched
(0.9861/0.9866).

== Regime delta vs the Azurite ladder (#1794) — why the ratio is lower

1. **Format regime.** The az ladder ran `develop @ 47bef62e`, which still wrote **v3 two-level
   segments** (14.4 GET/q cascade dominated by survivor ranges — the wave covered most of the RTT
   share → 2.78x at 100 ms). This campaign ran `develop @ 431f995e2`, i.e. after the TD-PAXRG-1
   single-layout collapse (`SEG_LAYOUT_VERSION=1`): the coarse A0 directory now lives in v1
   segments (verified: A0 extents at header offsets 56..72, cells 7+22/15+15/30 parse correctly),
   but the cascade carries **~22-26 GETs/query of which the wave covers ~2.1 rounds' worth** —
   footer/index/OID/metadata GETs remain sequential. Decomposition: A0(150 ms) 5373 ms vs
   A1(150 ms) 3686 ms saves ~1687 ms ≈ 11 GET-RTTs of the ~20 sequential-GET share, i.e. ~half the
   cascade is still unwaved.
2. **RTT-coupled transfer.** Toxiproxy's latency toxic also degrades bulk TCP throughput
   (window-limited), so the ~54-67 MB/query transfer term grows with RTT in BOTH arms, crushing
   the ratio's denominator. A real WAN (bandwidth-delay product decoupled) would not pay this
   exact term.
3. **Compute floor is unchanged** (370k-545k probed rows/query single-threaded rank), so the
   saturation regime the az ladder found is still the binding asymptote here.

== Consequences

* **Ingest path finding (incidental, landed in the harness):** the settle gate + current develop
  harness deadlocked on ANY current-layout bed — the harness required `header[4]==3` and
  `--require-layout-version 3` while the collapse made every new segment v1 with A0 folded in
  (a0_len > 0 is the authoritative presence test). Fixed in the harness commit in the same PR;
  this was a latent develop bug for every post-collapse bed, not an S3-specific one.
* **The next compounding lever sharpens:** extend wave coverage to the remaining ~11 unwaved
  sequential GETs of the v1 cascade (footer chunks, row-group index/OID, metadata), on top of the
  already-filed parallel-probed-rank lever.
* Default-flip question unchanged: INFLIGHT stays opt-in; this campaign adds S3-backend evidence
  for the wave's safety (parity/identity) but its measured benefit on the v1 layout is
  1.17-1.64x at 5.5-150 ms.
