= Embedding retrieval-quality diagnostics — 2026-08-20
:toc:

== Decision

Keep BGE base v1.5 at 480 content tokens / 72 overlap as the NFCorpus control. The token-aware
implementation is correct; smaller chunks expose a recall-versus-vector-count tradeoff, not a
general quality win. Do not enable equal-weight BM25+dense RRF by default from this evidence.

At 128/19, Recall@10 improves by `0.008894` (+5.00% relative) versus 480/72, and a deterministic
10,000-sample paired query bootstrap places the 95% interval at `[+0.000502, +0.018948]`. However,
NDCG@10, MRR@10, Hit@10 and AP@10 all move slightly down with intervals that include zero, while
the corpus grows from 4,145 to 15,200 vectors (3.67x) and float32 vector payload grows from
12,733,440 to 46,694,400 bytes. This is not a Pareto improvement.

== Question and controlled setup

The audit worked backwards from the customer outcome: retrieve enough correct evidence early,
within a bounded vector/storage/compute envelope. It tested whether low raw Recall@10 came from a
chunker defect, retrieval granularity, or dense-only retrieval.

All neural points share:

* official NFCorpus test split: 3,633 documents, 323 queries, 12,334 positive document qrels;
* `BAAI/bge-base-en-v1.5` revision
  `a5beb1e3e68b9ab74eb54cfd186867f64f240e1a`, 768 dimensions;
* role-correct BGE query instruction, unprefixed passages, normalized float32 embeddings;
* complete-source corpora, `deduplicate=none`, split overflow and zero truncation;
* recursive structural boundaries and approximately 15% token overlap;
* PyTorch/MPS embedding followed by exact normalized float32 inner product;
* maximum chunk score per source and macro document-qrels metrics.

The 128/256/384 runs retained 200 chunks/query before source collapse; every @10 comparison has
complete source candidate depth. The historical 480 run retained 100 chunks/query and has 85-100
source candidates/query, also complete through @20. Its prior @100 value is now explicitly marked
as a lower-bound diagnostic because only 8/323 queries retain 100 distinct sources.

No timing is claimed. The host was not quiet, and the MPS shard times are retained only as console
progress. Quality and exact scores are contention-invariant.

== Metric interpretation

NFCorpus has median 16, mean 38.19 and maximum 475 relevant documents/query. Therefore perfect
macro Recall@10 is only `0.614572`; BGE-480 raw Recall@10 `0.177765` is `0.289251` of that ceiling.
Raw Recall remains the standard metric. The evaluator now also reports the ceiling, capped recall,
ceiling-normalized recall, Hit, Precision, AP, MRR, NDCG, the relevance-set histogram and candidate
completeness. These diagnostics explain the denominator; they do not replace raw Recall/NDCG.

== Lexical and fusion controls

`score_bm25_corpus.py` is a sealed dependency-free Okapi BM25 control with `k1=0.9`, `b=0.4` and
the recorded `unicode_word_casefold_v1` analyzer. It is deliberately not called an official BEIR,
Lucene or Tantivy result. `fuse_source_runs.py` applies deterministic weighted reciprocal-rank
fusion to query-complete source runs.

[cols="1,1,1,1,1,1",options="header"]
|===
|Retriever |NDCG@10 |Recall@10 |MRR@10 |Hit@10 |AP@10
|BGE-480 |0.375665 |0.177765 |0.575832 |0.752322 |0.282889
|Reference BM25 |0.304513 |0.146411 |0.506735 |0.674923 |0.220285
|Equal BGE+BM25 RRF (`k=60`) |0.356674 |0.170268 |0.574899 |0.724458 |0.262697
|===

Equal RRF reduces NDCG by `0.018991` with paired 95% interval
`[-0.032766, -0.005340]`, and AP by `0.020192` with interval
`[-0.033993, -0.006662]`. Hybrid retrieval is a capability, not an automatic quality win. Do not
tune RRF weights on this test set; use a development split and then evaluate once on held-out data.

== Code findings

No defect explains the @10 result: zero truncation, complete source coverage, correct BGE role
rendering, normalized float32 vectors and exact scoring all hold. The audit did find and close a
reporting defect at deeper `k`: top-N chunks do not necessarily produce N distinct sources after
collapse. The evaluator now records completeness for every requested `k` and can fail closed with
`--require-complete-k`.

Per-query sidecars and `compare_retrieval_runs.py` make model/chunk/fusion comparisons paired and
reproducible. Source-level runs are first-class evaluator inputs. The BM25 and RRF controls emit
sealed manifests with analyzer/fusion parameters, input hashes and deterministic tie breaks.
