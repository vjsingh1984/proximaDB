== The ProximaDB Business Advantage

Translating technical strength into business value is our mission. Here’s what ProximaDB’s unique approach means for you:

*   **Massive Scale, Low Cost:** Handle terabyte-scale vector datasets on a single, economical machine. Our disk-first storage engine means you don't need a fleet of high-RAM servers, drastically lowering your operational costs.

*   **Build Trust with Data Provenance:** For enterprise RAG, compliance, and research, knowing your data's origin is critical. ProximaDB's integrated knowledge graph tracks data lineage, so every result can be traced back to its source. Build AI systems that are transparent and auditable, not black boxes.

*   **Uncompromised Accuracy:** Storing indexes on disk provides the flexibility to use larger, more accurate vector representations without the memory constraints of other systems. Get more precise, reliable results you can count on for critical applications.

*   **Scale with Your Cloud Storage:** ProximaDB is designed to grow with your data. It scales seamlessly by leveraging cost-effective cloud storage like Amazon S3, letting you build massive indexes without massive infrastructure bills.
