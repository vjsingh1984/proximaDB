== Business Use Cases

=== Sales & Marketing
- Semantic search across proposals, briefs, notes
- Account intelligence: similar wins, competitor patterns
- Content reuse with provenance and freshness scoring

=== Customer Support
- Unified KB + ticket graph → fewer escalations
- Journey graph to connect symptoms→root cause
- Agent assist with grounded suggestions

=== Risk & Fraud
- Similarity + graph rings for collusion and mule detection
- Identity resolution via property constraints & paths
- Alert triage with context and explanations

=== RAG for LLMs
- Hybrid vector+graph for retrieval and assembly
- Provenance links to sources and paths
- Policy checks before presentation
