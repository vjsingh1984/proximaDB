== ProximaDB for Decision Makers: Business Overview
:toc:

== What ProximaDB Enables

- Faster time‑to‑answer across search, support, analytics, and risk — powered by Vector, Graph, and SKS in one platform.
- Lower TCO via progressive search (Binary→INT8→PQ→FP32), SIMD acceleration, and storage‑aware engines.
- Safer, explainable AI through graph‑based provenance and policy constraints.

== Priority Use Cases

- Revenue: Sales content discovery, proposal reuse, account intelligence
- Service: Unified KB search, journey graph, root‑cause linking
- Risk/Fraud: Similarity + multi‑hop rings, identity resolution, policy checks
- RAG: Grounded retrieval with sources and graph‑assembled context

== Why Now (Market Context)

- LLM adoption demands trusted retrieval; pure vector search isn’t enough.
- Teams need observable, explainable systems that fit existing budgets & skills.
- A unified Vector+Graph platform simplifies integration and reduces vendor sprawl.

== Adoption Path (90‑Day Plan)

1) Weeks 1–2: Pilot on a focused use case; measure time‑to‑answer and precision.
2) Weeks 3–6: Expand datasets, add graph assembly, track CSAT/ROI.
3) Weeks 7–12: Harden ops (SLOs, metrics), scale‑out, and roll to adjacent teams.

== What Good Looks Like (KPIs)

- 30–60% faster answers; 20–40% lower compute/storage costs.
- 15–30% fewer escalations; measurable risk/fraud catch‑rate lift.
- Higher trust: provenance links and fewer hallucinations in LLM workflows.

== Fit & Risks

- Fit: Teams needing search+reasoning, explainable outcomes, and stepwise adoption.
- Risks: Data quality, taxonomy maturity, and org readiness. Mitigate via smaller pilots and clear owners.
