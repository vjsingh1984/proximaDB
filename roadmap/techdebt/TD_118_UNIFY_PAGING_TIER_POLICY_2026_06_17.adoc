= TD-118 Unify Paging/Cache Policy with Tier Policy; Split Cache from FileSystem Mechanism
:toc:
:toclevels: 3
:sectnums:

Status: Open / proposed +
Date: 2026-06-17 +
Owner: Storage, infrastructure

== Context

The design doc `docs/12-design/PROXIMADB_AS_LAKEHOUSE_NATIVE_PG_ENGINE_2026_06_17.md` §1.5/7 answers
the "two seams — consolidate or separate?" question by mapping ProximaDB onto PostgreSQL's proven
factoring: `smgr` (addressing/mechanism) vs `bufmgr` (cache + eviction policy) vs `fd.c` (resource
pooling). "Page a block into RAM" and "demote a cold segment to S3/Glacier" are the **same OS-paging
decision at two rungs of one hierarchy**. The recommendation: keep mechanism separate, consolidate the
policy brain, and split the cache out of the mechanism layer.

== Current State (grounded)

* Mechanism: `FileSystem` trait (`src/storage/persistence/filesystem/mod.rs`) — pure I/O over
  local/S3/Azure/GCS.
* But `UnifiedCachingFilesystem` (`src/storage/persistence/filesystem/caching_filesystem.rs:35`)
  **fuses** cache admission/eviction, metadata cache, prefetch and range optimization into that
  mechanism layer.
* Tier policy: `tier_policy_engine.rs` (`InfrastructureTier` Memory…CloudDeepArchive) decides
  long-term placement **independently** of the cache.
* No `BufferAccessStrategy`-style scan ring → a large cold S3 scan can evict the OLTP working set.
* `proximadb-cache` crate's `TenantCache` (byte-weighted, tenant-namespaced) is the consolidation base.

== Target Design

Three layers, not two: pure `FileSystem` mechanism; a `PagingCache` cache layer; and one policy brain
shared by the cache and the tier engine. `FileSystem` and the tier engine stay separate seams.

== Deliverables

1. Extract caching out of `UnifiedCachingFilesystem` into a `PagingCache` layer over `TenantCache`,
   leaving `FileSystem` as pure mechanism.
2. One `HierarchyPolicy` / `CacheTemperaturePolicy` trait (`on_access` / `should_evict` /
   `should_demote→tier`) consumed by **both** the paging cache and the tier engine, driven by one
   `(temperature, tenant SLA, cost)` model via the existing JSON `TierPolicy` + `LimitsResolver` DI
   seam (`PROXIMADB_CACHE_TIERS_PATH`).
3. A `BufferAccessStrategy`-style scan ring / cache-bypass for large sequential scans.

== Acceptance

* A large scan does not evict hot OLTP entries (scan-ring behavior).
* A single policy config drives both eviction and tier demotion.
* `FileSystem` carries no cache logic.

== Dependencies & Sequencing

Independent. Builds on the shipped `proximadb-cache` foundation crate.
